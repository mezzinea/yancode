Discover the program

Surfing for every level

Beginner surfer

Catch your first waves on the gentle beach breaks a 10-minute walk from the Medina. Learn to paddle, stand up and read the ocean, in a relaxed, positive atmosphere.

Intermediate surfer

Follow the open face of the wave, start turning left and right, and make the step from foam board to hard board with personal guidance.

Advanced surfer

Reef and point breaks within 30 minutes of Rabat, chosen for the day’s swell, including La Crique in Bouznika: long, peeling waves in a sheltered cove.

Meet your surf coach

Abdel Elharim

Founder · Former pro surfer · African champion · ISA & Moroccan Federation certified coach · Rip Curl team rider

Born in Rabat, Abdel made history as the first Moroccan to compete on the WSL QS Tour and the first ever invited to the legendary Pipeline Masters in Hawaii. He brings world-class knowledge, personal attention and a deep love for the ocean to every session, from complete beginners to competitive athletes.

Surf spots for all levels

Rabat is one of Morocco’s most underrated surf destinations: uncrowded, consistent waves and a wide variety of spots. From Mehdia beach in the north to Bouznika bay in the south, everything is within a 30-minute drive.

01

Beach breaks

Ideal for beginners and progression, a 10-minute walk from the Medina.

02

Reefs & points

Lefts, rights and slabs for more experienced surfers.

03

La Crique, Bouznika

Our hidden gem, the “uncrowded Imsouane”: long, peeling waves in a sheltered cove.

  • Sunny all year round, warm and pleasant climate
  • Consistent waves in every season
  • Waves even on small-swell days thanks to multiple exposures
  • An authentic surf scene, far from the crowds of the south

Giving back

Free surf lessons for kids

Through our surfing association and in partnership with a local non-profit, we offer free surf coaching to children from underprivileged backgrounds in the Rabat area. Surfing becomes a tool for confidence, education and community, with a strong “leave no trace” respect for the ocean.

Good to know

Do I need any surf experience?

No. Our lessons are designed for beginners and intermediates, and surf guiding is open to all levels, from motivated beginners to advanced riders.

Is the equipment included?

Yes. Surfboard and wetsuit are included in lessons, and provided if needed for surf guiding.

Where do we meet?

Lessons take place at Rabat beach, a 10-minute walk from the Medina. Surf guiding starts from Rabat Medina with round-trip transport to the best spot of the day.

When is the best season?

September to May, when the swell is most consistent. There are waves in every season, and Rabat is sunny all year round.

Can children and families join?

Absolutely. Our sessions are open to all ages and are a great activity to share as a family.

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, call us or email us with your dates, level and number of people. We’ll confirm the best time for the waves.

Ready to surf?

The waves of Rabat are waiting. Tell us your dates and level, we take care of the rest.
